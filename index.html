System information

The system continuously measures air temperature, relative humidity and wind speed under the waiting shed. An ESP32 microcontroller evaluates these readings every 2 seconds and switches the misting pump automatically. No manual operation is needed.

Automatic misting conditions

Temperature ≥ 31 °C
AND relative humidity < 70%
AND wind speed between 0.5 and 4.0 m/s

If any one condition is not satisfied, the pump remains OFF. The humidity limit was adjusted from 60% to 70% RH; the system now operates with the 70% limit.

Pump cycle

Pump ON
30 seconds
Pump OFF
3 minutes

After the OFF period, the environmental conditions are checked again. Because of this rest interval, the dashboard can show that all conditions are met while the pump is still resting.

Measured values

  • Temperature and humidity: the average of the valid readings from three DHT22 sensors.
  • Heat index: calculated on the ESP32 with the NOAA/NWS heat index method and displayed in °C.
  • Wind speed: measured by an analog anemometer. A wind event is recorded when the speed stays within 0.5–4.0 m/s.

Self-check indicator

Every 2 seconds the ESP32 checks the three DHT22 sensors, the anemometer, the real-time clock (DS3231) and the microSD card. A green indicator lamp flashes for 0.3 seconds only when all six pass. The lamp is separate from the pump relay and does not show whether the pump is running.

Data flow

ESP32→Wi-Fi→Supabase database→This website

Raw readings are uploaded about every 2 seconds. The database then summarizes them automatically into 1-minute, hourly, daily and monthly records, which feed the Historical data page. The original 2-second measurements are also kept on the microSD card as the experimental record. This website only reads data; it cannot change the system.

About the project

Design and Development of a Roof-Mounted Misting System to Provide Thermal Comfort in Outdoor Campus Spaces

Institution: Polytechnic University of the Philippines – Institute of Technology (PUP ITECH)
Author: LIBIRAN JR., HENRY MICHAEL S.

Overview

This thesis project is an automated environmental monitoring and misting system for an outdoor waiting shed at PUP ITECH. High-pressure misting nozzles are mounted on the roof of the shed, and an ESP32-based controller decides when they operate.

Purpose

The system aims to reduce thermal discomfort in outdoor campus spaces. It monitors environmental conditions, provides automated misting when temperature, relative humidity and wind speed indicate that cooling is appropriate, and records the data needed to evaluate thermal conditions at the site. The project seeks to support thermal comfort for students and other people who wait or rest in the shed.

This website

The website is a public, read-only view of the system. It shows current readings, the reason the pump is ON or OFF, the status of the monitoring hardware, and stored measurements at 1-minute, hourly, daily and monthly resolution.

Methods in brief

  • Three DHT22 sensors and an analog anemometer measure temperature, humidity and wind speed.
  • Heat index is computed with the NOAA/NWS method.
  • Readings are logged locally to a microSD card and uploaded to an online database for monitoring and historical analysis.
